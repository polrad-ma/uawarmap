Welcome

Welcome and thanks for using my map!

This map was created to show the situation in Ukraine. Of course, this map, like every other war map, is not 100% reliable or true.

Use the layers and the map tools featured here. Map updates are displayed via the bell icon on the left sidebar.

This map was inspired by @M0nstas and @Playfra0 and other mappers, and was designed mainly with Claude AI and Gemini AI. If used, please tag me, @polrad_mapping_, in your posts.

I use multiple sources to update and clarify the map — mainly geolocation data, other mappers, and partially my own sources.
